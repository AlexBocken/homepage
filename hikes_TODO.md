## 1. Lauerz → Rigi Hochflue + Rigi Scheidegg

https://www.gipfelbuch.ch/routen/29133--ab-lauerz-hochflue-und-scheidegg

- **Where:** Schwyz, north shore of the Lauerzersee, east end of the Rigi
  massif. Circular from Lauerz (458 m).
- **Highlights:** Gätterlipass → Rigi Scheidegg (1662 m, viewing platform) →
  Rigi Hochflue (1699 m). Ridge walking, Vierwaldstättersee/Mythen panorama.
  Crux: 25 m near-vertical ladder off the summit, chains on the south flank.
- **Numbers:** 19.7 km, 1832 m ascent, ~8 h walking.
- **Difficulty: T4** — hardest of the four. Route-finding needed, sparsely
  marked/unmarked sections, exposure. Not a wet-rock day.
- **Travel: 1 h 06.** Oerlikon 07:21 → S6 → Zürich HB → EC → Arth-Goldau →
  Bus 501 → Lauerz, Dorf **08:27**. (Also 06:21 → 07:27.)
  Return: Lauerz, Dorf 17:29 → Oerlikon 18:44.
- Best transport of the bunch, but 8 h + T4 makes it a full day. Start 07:27.

## 2. Gfellen → Pilatus ridge traverse

https://www.gipfelbuch.ch/routen/11749-pilatus-2118m-gfellen-mittagguepfi-widderfeld-tomlishorn-pilatus

- **Where:** Entlebuch (LU), north side of the Pilatus chain. One-way
  Gfellen → Pilatus Kulm.
- **Highlights:** Stäfeliflue, Blaue Tosse, Mittaggüpfi (1917 m), Widderfeld
  (2075 m), Tomlishorn (2128 m, highest point). Long ridge line, quiet until
  the last hour, then the tourist Kulm.
- **Numbers:** ~1800 m ascent, ~7 h.
- **Difficulty: T3.** Marked throughout; wooden stairs on an eroded slope, the
  chain-secured descent off Widderfeld is the crux. Exposed but secured.
- **Travel: 1 h 38.** Oerlikon 06:00 → Zürich HB → IR70 Luzern → RE7 Entlebuch
  → Bus 232 → Gfellen **07:38**.
  Return: Pilatus Kulm 17:00 → Fräkmüntegg gondola → Kriens → Luzern →
  Oerlikon 19:08 (~2 h 08; cogwheel to Alpnachstad also works).
- Descent by lift, so the 7 h is the whole effort. Check lift closing times
  before committing.

## 3. Surenenpass: Brüsti → Engelberg

https://uri.swiss/tour/surenenpass-bruesti-engelberg

- **Where:** Uri → Obwalden, crossing from Attinghausen to Engelberg.
- **Highlights:** Nossenboden, Alp Grat, Angistock, Surenenpass (2291 m, hut
  just below), Blackenalp, Stäuber waterfall, Fürenalp. Urnersee + Reusstal
  views on the way up, Titlis + Schächentaler Alps from the pass.
  Chamois/ibex likely.
- **Numbers:** 20.7 km, +806 / −1320 m, ~7 h — the cable cars do most of the
  climbing.
- **Difficulty: easy–medium (T2).** Long, but a classic pass crossing on good
  paths. The most "just walk" of the four.
- **Travel: 2 h 25.** Oerlikon 05:46 → IR13 → Zürich HB → IR46 Altdorf →
  Bus 412 → Attinghausen, Seilbahn **08:11**, then the Brüsti cable car.
  Return: Engelberg 17:02 → Luzern → Oerlikon 19:08.
- Longest approach. Check the Brüsti cable-car first ascent and the Fürenalp
  last descent (or walk out to Engelberg).

## 4. Churfirsten-Weg (SchweizMobil local route 950)

https://switzerlandmobility.ch/en/hiking-in-switzerland/route-950

- **Where:** Obertoggenburg (SG), over the top of the Churfirsten chain.
  Selamatt → Gamsalp.
- **Highlights:** Hinterrugg (2306 m, highest Churfirst) → Chäserrugg (Herzog &
  de Meuron summit restaurant, Walensee dropping away 2000 m to the south) →
  Gamserrugg → Gamsalp.
- **Numbers:** 10 km, +1100 / −720 m, ~4 h 35. Shortest by far.
- **Difficulty: medium (≈T2/T3), "difficult" fitness** — 1100 m of climb packed
  into 10 km.
- **Travel: 2 h 13.** Oerlikon 06:33 → S7 Rapperswil → S4 Wattwil → Bus 790 →
  Alt St. Johann (Sellamattbahn) **08:46**; Sellamatt cable car up. Down from
  Gamsalp by chairlift to Wildhaus Oberdorf → bus.
  Return: Wildhaus, Dorf 17:02 → Oerlikon 19:08.
- Half a day of walking, and two lifts do the vertical. Good short /
  uncertain-weather pick; both lifts are mandatory unless you want +500 m /
  −900 m more on foot.

## 5. Bruderklausenweg (SchweizMobil local route 571)

https://schweizmobil.ch/de/wanderland/route-571

- **Where:** Nidwalden → Obwalden, Stans to Sachseln, along the Jakobsweg over
  the ridge between the two valleys.
- **Highlights:** The Niklaus von Flüe pilgrim route — Kerns, then the Ranft
  gorge with the hermitage and chapels where Brother Klaus lived, Flüeli-Ranft,
  ending at the parish church in Sachseln that holds his relics. Chapels and
  farmland the whole way, Sarnersee at the end. Inaugurated 1981 for the 500th
  anniversary of the Peace of Stans, renovated 2017.
- **Numbers:** 21 km, +840 / −820 m, ~5 h 30 – 6 h 30.
- **Difficulty: easy (T1), "difficult" fitness** — no alpine terrain at all,
  but a long day on foot. Snow-free far longer than the others.
- **Travel: 1 h 18.** Oerlikon 07:00 → Zürich HB → IR70 Luzern → S4 →
  Stans **08:18**. (Also 06:00 → 07:18.)
  Return: Sachseln 17:29 → Luzern → Oerlikon 19:08 (1 h 39).
- Best transport of the lot and no lifts to catch. The shoulder-season /
  bad-forecast fallback.
